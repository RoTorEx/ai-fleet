Leaves when you open the session or it starts new workClear

History is one more chip on the existing filter row: a clock and a count, because the row has no room for another word. It holds only alerts you have not dealt with, one per session: an alert leaves History, the screen and Notification Center once its session is opened, starts new work or ends. Alerts that need you stay on top with a live waiting time; a row click is the same jump as the banner. Rows show text only when the text setting is on. Nothing is written to disk, which keeps the current rule that session text is never persisted by Fleet.

7 · Alternatives

Two choices worth seeing side by side

Layout A · recommended
Layout B · quiet meta
Glyphs as plain dots

A keeps type and session on one line and leaves two body lines for text. B gives the type its own line and demotes meta to regular weight, at the cost of one body line. This board assumes macOS draws both title and subtitle bold (to be confirmed live); if so, only B makes the meta visually quiet. Dots are calmer but lean on colour alone; shapes stay readable for colour-blind eyes and in a stack.

8 · Decisions

Decisions, limits and one dependency

Decisions · accepted as recommended

  1. Layout: A or B. Recommended: A, confirmed on real banners with preview.sh.
  2. Glyph set: shapes (✋ 💬 ⛔ ✅ 🪫 🔔) or dots. Recommended: shapes.
  3. Text order: lead with the agent's text for Question and Blocked. Recommended: yes.
  4. “Show message text” default: Recommended: off, since it changes where session text is stored.
  5. History: in memory, only alerts not dealt with yet; an alert is withdrawn when its session is opened, starts new work or ends. Changed on review from “last 50”.

System limits and dependency

  1. To verify live: how many body lines the collapsed banner shows, and the subtitle weight. This board assumes two lines and bold. Run design/notifications/preview.sh to see the real thing.
  2. Buttons appear under the system's Options control on hover and as full buttons when expanded; their look is not ours to style.
  3. No colour, no custom layout in a system banner. A custom expanded view needs a notification content extension; a fully custom panel loses Notification Center and Focus. Both stay out of this version.
  4. Dependency: the session engine reports that an approval is pending, not what is being approved. Until it captures the command, Approval shows the request and the agent's last message.
  5. Kimi and providers without a bound transcript have no text; their alerts always use the fixed sentence.
