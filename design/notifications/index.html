3 · Every alert

Closed and open, on the real line budget

A finished reply answers a request, so Done is a dialogue: a little of your request on one line, then the reply, cut at 190 characters. A question, a blocker and an approval are the agent speaking alone, up to 250 characters. Blank lines are counted: the closed banner then shows the request and the first reply line.

4 · Limits and decisions

What we accept, what was decided

Accepted limits

  1. Ten lines is the ceiling. A longer or styled view needs a notification content extension: a separate signed app extension that Fleet's build cannot produce today. Not taken.
  2. No conversation history in a banner. It holds one exchange: your request and the agent's last text. Notification Center keeps one entry per session.
  3. Kimi has no reply text: no bound transcript, so Done carries only the place. A turn stopped by a provider error shows that error line.
  4. Approval cannot name the action yet: it shows the agent's last message instead.

Decided 2026-11-22

  1. Scheme: glyph, provider and project in the title; the name on its own line; the place as the last paragraph: named window and tab.
  2. Text on by default, as a dialogue with air: 👤: one request line, 🤖: the reply, blank lines between. Needs-you alerts carry the agent alone.
  3. No buttons, no History list in Fleet. A click on the banner opens the pane; the list only repeated the banner inside the app.
  4. Quota glyph by level: 🔋 above 25% left, 🪫 at and below.
